## A. Bond capacity counts trades that hold no money (blocker)

Classic create, 27,000 sats, ⛓ On Bitcoin → "The largest eligible bond
here covers up to 0 sats today." At 15:00 the same bond covered a 30,000
listing. What changed is what got counted: `getOpenBondedTrades`
(`arbiters/exposure.ts:153`) treats every trade as exposure unless its
status is COMPLETED or CANCELLED (`TRULY_TERMINAL_STATES`, `types.ts:68`).
So EXPIRED listings where Bitcrazy was seated, RESOLVED trades never
marked complete, and CREATED listings nobody funded all eat the bond,
forever. Yesterday's tests alone are enough to reach 100k. Same reason
the 27k ecash listing got BitcoinSafariTz (unbonded) as arbiter: Bitcrazy
was "full".

Rule: a bond is exposed only where sats are actually held under that
arbiter's key — status LOCKED, APPROVED or CLAIMED (and for on-chain, a
committed deposit that hasn't been spent). CREATED, EXPIRED, RESOLVED-
then-settled and anything terminal count zero. `isOpenExposure` changes
accordingly; `TRULY_TERMINAL_STATES` stays what it is (it has other
users).

The error box lists what is using the bond when it refuses: "Bitcrazy's
bond is covering: 30,000 sats (trade …abc, locked), 27,000 sats (…def,
approved)". A seller can then see whether waiting or lowering helps.

Tests: EXPIRED with seated arbiter → 0 exposure; LOCKED → full amount;
COMPLETED → 0; a range listing counts its max only once LOCKED at the
chosen amount.

## B. Fiat under the range, on the question and on the summary

Guided sell, "How much Bitcoin are you selling?": under each bracket
amount show its fiat at the live median — "≈ 0.02 USD" under 21, "≈ 50.55
USD" under 60,000 — updating as they type, same source as the price
card. Summary "Amount: 21–60,000 sats" gets a second line "≈ 0.02–50.55
USD at today's rate". Same on the classic form's range fields if it has
them.

## C. On-chain in the guided sell flow, ranges included

The guided sell flow never shows the on-chain choice for a range because
`offeredSats` (`AssistedCanvas.tsx:579`) reads the low end. Rule: show the
⛓ / ⚡ choice when `rangeMax ≥ 25,000`; preselect on-chain when
`rangeMin ≥ 100,000`. When on-chain is chosen and `rangeMin < 25,000`,
say under the low field: "On-chain needs at least 25,000 sats per trade —
raise the low end or choose Chama ecash", and don't publish until it is.
Same fee checkout as the classic form, computed at the high end.

## D. "Public history unavailable" is not a seat chip line

`b4fff509` prints "Public history unavailable." on seat chips and listing
cards for every key without new-style proofs, and "No verified bond found
for BitcoinSafariTz" in the arbiter box. Per the proof-of-conduct skill,
unknown is not zero, but it is also not an error message. Seat chips and
cards show nothing when there is nothing; the record card says "No public
history yet." An unbonded arbiter reads "BitcoinSafariTz has no bond" —
and after A that seat should be rare.
