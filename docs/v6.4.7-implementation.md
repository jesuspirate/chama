## 03 — durable recording; stopped before reconciliation

The raw SDK has optional `federation.getOperation` and `subscribeLnReceive`,
and the browser adapter resumes recent pending receives on startup. This is
not a shared authoritative `receiveOutcome(operationId)` contract: the public
wallet interface exposes only receive creation and outgoing-pay outcome
queries. In particular it cannot prove expired-and-unpaid across supported
browser/native wallets. No reconciliation result is invented from balance,
local deadlines, or a bounded recent-operation scan.

Step 1 records every issued funding invoice before the QR/auto-payer sees it,
including its operation id when the gateway callback supplies one, and captures
its original identity/federation. Aborts/failures mark abandonment; a killed
page leaves a durable watching record. Late creation after timeout is recorded
on the original promise. Storage is verified before invoice creation and before
display. Corruption/blocked storage fails closed; no TTL, cap or delete exists.
Even a successful lock only records an observation, not proof of the invoice's
terminal wallet outcome. The original durable record survives status-write
failure after a possibly committed lock.

Steps 2–4 remain blocked behind that receive-outcome contract and funded-wallet
reconciliation tests. No recovered-payment banner, competing funding run, or
promise that late sats are spendable was added. The live-invoice Switch guard
and its existing honest explanation remain unchanged.
