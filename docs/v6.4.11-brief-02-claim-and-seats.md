# v6.4.11 brief 02 — The claim button says what happens; seats show who

Jet, 2026-09-28, APK 6.4.10 (Bitcrazy claiming 196 sats to getalby).
Normies-simplification skill applies to A. Commit each; don't push or ship.

## A. The rail button names the outcome, not the rail

`ClaimPayoutModal.tsx:808`: the big button under the rail picker reads
"LN · FAST" / "ONCHAIN · SLOW" / "Ecash · no fees". It is the button that
performs the claim, so it must say what tapping it does, with the
destination in it:

- Lightning, saved wallet selected: **"Send 196 sats to bitcrazy@getalby.com"**
- Lightning, nothing selected yet: **"See all Lightning options"** → opens
  the address / invoice / NWC / LNURL choices (the picker that exists
  today), then the button becomes the sentence above.
- On-chain: **"Paste a Bitcoin address"** → field appears → **"Send 196
  sats to bc1q…"**.
- Ecash: **"Get an ecash note"** → then the import screen as today.

Speed/fee hints move to one muted line under the rail row, never into
the button: "Lightning · seconds · Bitcoin · ~1 hour · Ecash · no fee".
Same treatment in the funding card's method labels where the pattern
repeats. All four languages.

## B. Seats show who is who, not a dot

The seat chips (`LiveTradeSurface.tsx:~938`, `ProfileAvatar` at 24px
with a coloured dot) don't read at a glance: a blank person icon with a
tiny blue or orange dot. Make the role visible on every chip:

- The avatar ring takes the role colour (buyer / seller / arbiter — the
  same colours the chat labels use), 2px, on every chip including "open
  seat" (dashed ring, no fill).
- No photo → the Chama wordmark circle in the role colour as the
  fallback, not the generic person outline.
- Photo → the photo inside the coloured ring.
- The dot goes; presence ("ready", "just here", "quiet") stays as text.

Same chips in the full trade view's parties list and on the attention
card, so a role looks the same everywhere. Check light and dark.
