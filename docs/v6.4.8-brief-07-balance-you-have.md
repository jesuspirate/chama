# Brief 07 — sats already in your wallet should be visible, and spendable

Jet, 2026-09-24: *"if the user has a balance out of that [a late payment],
can they create a new trade and lock with that same balance, or is that
blocked because Chama expects the user to withdraw it?"*

Answer today: **neither, quite — and small amounts are invisible.** This
brief fixes both. Verified against `2d613b3b`.

## What happens to a late payment today

A funding invoice pays into the payer's **own** Chama wallet; locking is a
second step. If the lock never happens, the sats stay as wallet balance.
Nothing is lost. But:

1. **It isn't shown under 2,000 sats.** The Chama bar's stranded pill and the
   Me leftover card both wait for `MATERIAL_RECOVERY_MIN_SATS = 2_000`
   (`lightning-fees.ts:54`); below that the leftover "stays hidden and
   silently accumulates" by design (`MeScreen.tsx` ~713). And the Wallet tab
   (`MeScreen.tsx:1010`) shows **no balance at all** — only Withdraw ecash,
   payment methods and Lightning addresses. A 100-sat late payment is in the
   wallet with nothing on any screen saying so.
2. **It can't fund the next trade.** Fund & lock on a new trade always asks
   for a fresh invoice for the full amount. The only path that locks from
   existing balance is the recovery shortcut for the *same* escrow
   (`useEscrow.ts` ~4693: re-absorbed attempt or intent-stage crash window).
   So a user holding 500 sats who joins a 100-sat trade pays 100 more, and
   the 500 keep sitting there unseen.

It isn't blocked on purpose. It was never built.

## Build

### 1. Show the number

The Wallet tab's first row is the balance, plainly: *"In your Chama wallet:
₿ 500"*, with the existing sats⇄fiat rocker. Zero shows as zero. This is not
an alert and carries no colour — the 2,000-sat recovery threshold stays as
the line for *alerting*; it must not also be the line for *seeing*.

### 2. Define spendable, once

Not all balance is free. The pill already knows two things that can own it:
a pending native lock (`hasPendingNativeLock`) and an unfinished claim
payout (`hasPendingClaimPayout`). Add a pure
`spendableBalanceMsats(balance, pendingLocks, pendingPayouts)` in
`decisions.ts` that subtracts every earmarked amount, never going below zero,
and use it everywhere this brief touches. If a earmark's amount is unknown,
spendable is zero — fail closed.

### 3. Lock from balance when it covers the trade

When `spendable ≥ trade amount`, Fund & lock opens on **Lock from your
balance** — *"Use ₿ 100 of your ₿ 500"* — above the Lightning QR, one tap, no
invoice. Reuse the direct-lock mechanics that already exist at
`useEscrow.ts` ~4693, including their two honest refusals (trade no longer
CREATED; lock not confirmed by `notesHash`). The Lightning QR stays available
under it for anyone who'd rather pay fresh.

When spendable is less than the trade, the card behaves exactly as today.
Partial top-up (pay only the difference) is out of scope for this pass.

### 4. Write the stale-invoice line truthfully

Brief 05 §5 proposes *"Paid an older request by mistake? It lands in your
wallet."* That line only becomes true to the user once §1 ships. Land them
together.

## Tests

- Spendable subtracts pending locks and payouts; unknown earmark ⇒ zero.
- Lock-from-balance appears only when spendable ≥ amount, and never on a
  non-CREATED trade.
- A balance-funded lock creates no invoice and no journal entry.
- The Wallet tab renders the balance at 0, at 100 and at 5,000 sats, with no
  alert styling at any of them.

## Verification

Typecheck, tests, build. Then in `?sim=1`: fund 500, abandon a trade, open a
new 100-sat trade, and lock it from balance with no invoice shown.
