# v6.4.10 brief 09 — The payout has its own txid

First mainnet on-chain trade settled 2026-09-26 18:02 (`sm_muir4bz7_4pn9jfv1`).
On the completed screen, "Payout sent · waiting for confirmation" and
"See it on-chain" both point at the **funding** transaction
(`90b36c8f…`), not the payout. `OnchainEscrowPanel.tsx:240-249` only knows
`view.fundingTxid`; the reducer's COMPLETE handler verifies the final
settlement proof (`state-machine.ts:~1520`) and gets `proof.txid`, then
throws it away.

Commit; don't push or ship.

1. `handleComplete` (on-chain): store `next.onchainPayoutTxid = proof.txid`
   (cooperative or arbitrated). Replay-pure — it comes from the signed
   settlement message, not from the network. Also keep the winner's
   destination (`payoutAddress` or the per-trade key address) as
   `next.onchainPayoutAddress`.
2. `deriveOnchainView`: expose `payoutTxid` and `payoutAddress`. In stages
   `settling` and `done`, "See it on-chain" links to the payout txid; the
   funding txid keeps its own smaller link "Deposit ↗".
3. Completed screen (guided and full): "Payout sent · waiting for
   confirmation · 6d1f…" → after the explorer reports ≥1 confirmation,
   "Payout confirmed · 29,8xx sats to bc1q…". Poll the txid status every
   60 s until confirmed, then stop. The "Trade payout" notification links
   to the payout txid too.
4. Me → My trades row for a completed on-chain trade shows the payout
   txid short form, not the deposit's.

Test: a completed on-chain chain replays with `onchainPayoutTxid` equal to
the txid of the final settlement PSBT; the view links to it; a trade
completed before this change (no stored txid) still renders with the
deposit link only.

## The green card, and the flicker (same root)

The green "YOUR ON-CHAIN PAYOUT" block on the completed screen is
`OnchainPayoutRecoveryCard` (embedded) — the card for sweeping a payout
that landed on the winner's **per-trade Chama key**. Here the payout went
straight to Bestie's Fedi address, so that output will never exist. Two
consequences Jet saw:

- Only the winner gets the card (Jetty sees nothing) — the screens differ.
- `OnchainPayoutRecoveryCard.tsx:~70`: while the output is absent it
  re-scans **every 3 seconds, forever**, and each scan re-renders the
  card (loading → "Checking…" → result). That is the whole-page "glitch".

5. Render the recovery card only when the completed trade's payout
   destination is the per-trade key (`onchainPayoutAddress` equals the
   winner's derived address, or no direct address was chosen). For a
   direct payout, both sides get the same payout status line from item 3,
   nothing green and nothing to sweep.
6. When the recovery card does apply: keep the last result on screen while
   refreshing (no "Checking…" swap), back off 3 s → 10 s → 30 s → 60 s,
   stop once the output is spendable or after the payout txid confirms.
   Nothing on a completed screen polls faster than once a minute.
