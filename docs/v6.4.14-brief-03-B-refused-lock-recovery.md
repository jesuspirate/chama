# Refused LOCK funding

The relay ACK establishes that the late LOCK was transported. It does not
establish escrow custody. After A, the 170 sats are excluded from the committed
trade. The reported Wallet balance of 4 sats is consistent with an out-of-band
170-sat spend whose original bearer notes remain in the funder's device-local
pending-lock stash. Shares do not destroy the funder's original bearer copy.

The room now offers “Take your 170 sats back” for that exact saved note/hash,
owned by the refused LOCK's author. The action loads and replays the trade again,
checks the matching saved note, and uses the existing mutex-protected ecash
self-reissue path. No CLAIM or replacement LOCK is published. Successful credit
clears the saved funding record, refreshes Wallet, and removes the action.
Needs-you prioritizes this refund even after the listing or buyer hold expires.

The background drain retains this refusal until the person takes the action.
Unknown trade reads, a different federation, a summary, a different note hash,
a committed LOCK or CLAIM do not authorize this refund. A dead-note error alone
is not proof of wallet credit; this action keeps its recovery record on that
error. The ordinary crash-recovery decision table remains otherwise unchanged.

`node --import tsx src/fedimint/rejected-lock-recovery.tests.tsx` verifies the
seven-event refusal with a saved local spend, room copy, needs-you priority,
expiry visibility, and 4 → 174 sats in an injected wallet after one self-reissue.
It also verifies wrong owner/hash/federation, missing positive refusal and
unconfirmed credit stay closed. Typecheck passes.

This is an implementation and offline wallet-path test, not a live redemption
receipt. We have no access to the Pixel's wallet database or saved bearer copy.
If the old JOIN-first replay already caused recovery to delete that copy, the
new code cannot recreate it from one holder-only share. The native spend
operation ID is retained only in that same stash, not a separate spend recovery
API. No balance, mint credit, or presence of the original note on the Pixel is
claimed as independently verified. App publication was explicitly withheld.

The refund specifically uses `reabsorbBearerNotes` with measured credit, through
a dedicated `redeemRejectedNotes` dependency. Legacy `redeemWithRetry` success
cannot substitute for credit evidence. Missing credit verification keeps the
bearer copy; regression coverage checks that boundary too.
