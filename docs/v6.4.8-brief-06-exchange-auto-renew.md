# Brief 06 — Exchange offers renew while their seller keeps showing up

Jet, 2026-09-23: *"let users naturally renew automatically in Exchange IF
they keep opening the app weekly… Maybe our fear of spam is unreasonable?"*
Then: *"go on auto-renew."* Verified against `2d613b3b`.

## Most of this already exists — and one gate switches it off

The renewal machinery is built and careful (`src/escrow-engine/
listing-renewal.ts`, the retired-id ledger, the #74/#75 duplicate cleanup,
the #82 age-out). And the policy already says what Jet wants:
`resolveRenewalPolicy` gives the **exchange** lane
`autoRenew: true, requiresBond: false`, a 7-day tenure
(`EXCHANGE_TENURE_SECONDS`), bill and work 30 days. Only the **store** lane
requires a bond.

But the effect that actually renews, `App.tsx` ~1698, opens with:

```ts
if (!connected || !pubkey || !sellerBonded || !storeAutoRenewEnabled) return;
```

Every lane is gated on the **store** conditions — a verified bond *and* the
opt-in "Keep my offers live" toggle. So the exchange policy's "no bond" is
dead code: no unbonded seller has ever had an Exchange offer auto-renew.

## Build

### 1. Gate by lane, not globally

Keep `connected && pubkey` as the outer gate — a renewal is a fresh CREATE
the seller signs, so it can only happen while their app is open. That is the
liveness signal Jet asked for, and it is structural, not a rule we enforce:
a seller who stops opening the app stops renewing, and their offers lapse
within one 24h window.

Inside, decide per listing with `resolveRenewalPolicy(l, { bonded })`:

- **store** — unchanged. Bond + the toggle, exactly as today.
- **exchange / bill / work** — renew when the policy says `autoRenew`, no
  bond, no toggle, on by default.

Keep every existing guard on the path: the retired ledger, the in-session
ref, the per-pass cap of five, the age-out, the duplicate cleanup.

### 2. What "keeps opening the app weekly" means, concretely

`canRenewListing` already accepts a listing that is lapsed **or** inside the
15-minute lead window. So:

- Seller's app open near lapse → renewed before it ever disappears.
- Seller opens the app two days later → the lapsed offer is renewed on open.
- Seller gone for good → nothing signs a renewal; the offer lapses and stays
  gone.
- Age-out (#82) stops a lineage after its cycle cap (about 7 for Exchange)
  when nobody ever showed interest. Interest resets it.

That is "weekly" without a weekly timer: presence renews, absence lapses,
indifference ages out.

### 3. The one new rule — don't renew a ghost

Tonight's failure (brief 03) was exactly this: a live-looking offer, a buyer
joined, the seller didn't lock in time. Auto-renew must not turn one ghost
offer into a week of them.

**If a buyer took a seat on a lineage and the seller's lock window lapsed
without a lock, that lineage stops auto-renewing** until the seller renews it
by hand. One missed buyer is forgivable; the manual renew is the seller saying
"I'm actually here". Record it on the lineage (`listingIdentityKey`) next to
the auto-renew count, and show it on the seller's own listing: *"Paused — a
buyer waited and you didn't lock. Renew to show it again."*

### 4. Say it to the seller

On the "Your offer is live" screen and on the seller's listing: *"Stays live
while you use Chama. If you're away a day, it pauses."* One line. No toggle
for Exchange unless Jet asks for one.

## Is the spam fear reasonable?

Mostly no, and here's why in facts rather than feelings: every renewal is
signed by the seller's own key on the seller's own device, so it cannot
happen without them present; at most five renew per pass; lineages age out
without interest; duplicates collapse; and with rule 3 a seller who leaves a
buyer hanging is taken out of rotation. The residual risk is a determined
person keeping a tab open to hold offers they won't honour — which rule 3
catches the first time a buyer joins.

## Tests

- An unbonded seller's lapsed Exchange offer renews when the app is online;
  an unbonded store offer does not.
- A lapsed offer whose lineage recorded a missed lock does not auto-renew
  until a manual renew.
- The existing age-out, retired-ledger and dedupe tests still pass unchanged.
- No renewal fires while `connected` is false.

## Verification

Typecheck, tests, build. Then two browsers: post an Exchange offer, let it
lapse with the app closed, reopen — it renews once, with one new id and the
old retired. Join it from the other identity, let the lock window lapse —
the next lapse does not renew.
