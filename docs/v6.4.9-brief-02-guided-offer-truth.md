Scope: two fixes to the guided "cash → sats" results screen. Nothing else.

## Evidence (rehearsal, Jetty buying up to 2 USD via Strike)

- Card A: Bestie's Bill Pay "Utilities", 2,459 sats for 2 USD. Badges:
  BEST OVERALL · LOWEST PRICE · MOST TRUSTED · BILL PAY.
- Card B: Bitcrazy's "Sats for sale", 3,183 sats for 2 USD. No badges.
- Same money, card B gives ~29% more sats, yet card A is labelled
  "Lowest price".

## A. Price means fiat per sat, not fiat amount

`src/guided/match-listings.ts`:

- `priceScores` (≈line 106) and `recommendGuidedCandidates` →
  `lowestPrice` (≈line 296) compare `fiatQuote.amount`. Two $2 offers
  tie, so the badge falls to whatever scores best elsewhere.
- Compare the unit price instead: `fiatQuote.amount / amountSats`
  (lower = better for someone buying sats). For a ranged
  `exchange-bracket` item use the same ratio the card already uses
  (`quote.amount` corresponds to `candidate.amountSats`; see `Match` in
  `AssistedCanvas.tsx`).
- Keep the existing rules: same currency only, existing tiebreaks after
  the ratio, no badge when nothing is priced.
- The live budget filter (`visibleMatches`, AssistedCanvas ≈line 626)
  stays on the fiat amount; that is correct for "Up to X".

Tests: $2 / 2,459 vs $2 / 3,183 → 3,183 wins Lowest price and gets the
higher price score; $2 / 2,000 vs $3 / 3,000 → tie on ratio, falls
through to existing tiebreak; mixed currencies never compared.

## B. A Bill Pay result says it is a bill, and competes with its own kind

Today a Bill Pay listing is rendered exactly like a seller under
"Here's who you can buy from" and can collect every lane badge.

1. Lanes: **Best overall** and **Lowest price** compare `p2p-trade`
   offers only. A `bill-pay` candidate appears through its own
   `billOpportunity` lane (tag "Bill Pay"). **Most trusted** is about the
   person, so it may still land on a bill-pay candidate if it truly wins.
   If the only candidates are bill-pay, Best overall may fall back to the
   top bill-pay candidate so the screen is never badge-less.
2. Card copy for `category === "bill-pay"` (`Match` in
   `AssistedCanvas.tsx`): lead with the job, not the sats. English:
   "Pay a 2 USD Utilities bill" as the title line, "Get 2,459 sats" as
   the amount, the listing description as the small line. Normal sellers
   stay as they are.
3. Heading `canvas.chooseTitle` ("Here's who you can buy from.") is wrong
   when a bill appears. Use a neutral heading for the mixed/bill case,
   e.g. "Here's how you can get sats." Keep the current one when every
   card is a seller.
4. Review step for a bill-pay pick: check the rows read correctly (what
   Jetty pays, to whom, what he receives). Fix copy only if it says
   "buy from" or implies he pays the person rather than the bill.

All new strings in en, es, fr, sw.

Tests: a bill-pay candidate never carries Best overall / Lowest price
when a p2p offer exists; bill-pay card renders the "Pay a … bill" line;
heading switches only when a bill-pay card is visible.
