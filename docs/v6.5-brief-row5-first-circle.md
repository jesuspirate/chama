# v6.5 — Row 5: the first full circle (the final boss)

Written 2026-10-01, after 6.4.18 closed the notification loop on both
ends and Pass 0 completed on devices. Protocol: `docs/v6.5-acceptance-
protocol.md` §Row 5. Specs: `docs/chama-host-seat-spec.md` (ring, host
locks last), `docs/chama-rotation-v2-spec.md` (commitment round +
collection rounds, weekly race, collector sits out). Brief 8 of
`docs/v6.5-brief.md` precondition 5 says it exactly: *a real cycle has
run end-to-end in sim on more than one device, with the host locking
last and a collection round completing.* Sim is the design, not a
shortcut: money is fake, relays and signatures are real.

Read `.agents/skills/chama-bar/SKILL.md` and `proof-of-conduct` first.

## What must be true before anyone taps

1. **A "next" build, not a flag flip.** `CHAMA_RING_WRITER_ENABLED`
   and `CHAMA_ROTATION_ENABLED` stay `false` in the shipped build.
   Codex adds one build-time switch, `VITE_CHAMA_NEXT=1`, that sets
   both to `true`, forces sim mode on (every visit behaves as
   `?sim=1`; `?sim=0` is ignored), narrows the relay list to
   `wss://relay.chama.community` only, and stamps the client tag as
   `chama-next <version>`. The build is served from its own origin
   (`next.getchama.app` or `getchama.app/next/`, Jet's call; the
   release script gets `--deploy-next` and never touches the live
   path). Settings shows a permanent "NEXT · sim · relay.chama.community"
   badge in the header so nobody mistakes it for the real app.
2. **Old readers never see it.** Test (must fail against a build
   without the fix if there is one): a sim-tagged share-v2 CREATE and
   a ring share, fed to a client with sim off, are dropped at ingest
   (`shouldDropForSimPolicy`) before the parser can reject them. No
   thin circle view on a 6.4.x reader from anything the next build
   publishes.
3. **Cycle-failure and sacrifice-round copy exists** in en/es/fr/sw
   before the test, not after: what a failed fill means (cycle ends,
   refunds, standing minted for those who locked), what a post-
   collection default means (the mark), what "the collector sits out"
   means. Brief 8 requires this before the flip; the test is where it
   gets read by a human for the first time.
4. **The round watcher is on in the next build** (rotation spec engine
   delta 5): auto-vote RELEASE for the collector's payday after a
   successful fill, auto-vote REFUND after a failed fill, both
   mechanical, both logged to the Alert log with the round id.
5. **Clocks you can wait for.** On the next build only, the round
   length and the collect window are settable at circle creation down
   to 10 minutes (`COLLECT_WINDOW_SEC` stays 7 days in the shipped
   law). A week-long weekly race is the product; a ten-minute one is
   the test.

## The test (two devices, three identities)

Devices: the Mac (Brave, host identity H) and the Pixel 8 (Vanadium
or Chrome on the next origin, member identity M1). A third identity M2
in a second browser profile on the Mac, because a circle of two has no
race. Free the Pixel's storage first (it was at 2 %); a crash during
this test is evidence about the phone, not the circle, and we don't
want to argue about it.

1. H creates a circle: 3 seats, share 1,000 sim-sats, round length 10
   min, collect window 10 min. Record the circle id.
2. M1 (Pixel) and M2 join. Each device's seat row must show the same
   three seats, same states, after each action completes — not after
   a refresh. Screenshot each device after each step.
3. **Commitment round.** M1 locks, M2 locks, **H locks last.** The host
   seat must be refused until both members are in (`canTakeSeat`),
   and the moment H locks the ring is sealed: all three devices show
   "sealed" and the rotation order (round-1 LOCK timestamps, host
   last) identically. Pixel notifications for each lock with the app
   swiped away.
4. **Round 1, the race.** Round 1 opens at the commitment round's
   end. The collector for round 1 is whoever `collectorForRound`
   says; the two others lock their share-v2 escrows to the collector
   within the fill window; the collector sits out. Fill completes →
   the round watcher auto-votes RELEASE from roundEndSec → the
   collector gets "✅ your N×1,000 sats are ready" (sim) and claims
   through the durable-claim queue; the payers see "Resolved — the
   sats go to {collector}". Record every LOCK and VOTE timestamp.
5. **Round 2 opens immediately** at round 1's roundEndSec ("mining a
   block kickstarts the race"). Run it the same way with the next
   collector. Two completed collection rounds are enough; the cycle
   does not need to finish for Row 5.
6. **One failed fill, deliberately.** Create a second circle, seal it,
   and in round 1 let one payer not lock. After the fill window: the
   watcher auto-votes REFUND, the locked payer is refunded, standing
   is minted for those who locked and not for the no-show, the cycle
   ends, and every device says so in the user's language with the
   copy from §3.
7. **Cold replay.** On a fourth, fresh client (private window, sim on,
   next origin) run `inspect-trade-relays.ts` against the circle id
   and each round's share ids. Every chain replays to the same state
   the devices showed, with no healing, no "ignored events" note
   that isn't benign, and no manual step.

FAIL if any step needed a refresh to become correct, a heal, a manual
vote the watcher should have cast, or produced a replay note that is
not benign. FAIL if the host could lock before the members. FAIL if a
6.4.x client on the live origin shows anything from this test.

Record in `docs/v6.5-readiness.md` under Row 5: both devices, the
circle ids, share amount, round and window lengths, every lock/vote
timestamp, the failed-fill outcome, the cold-replay output. Then Brief
8's five preconditions are a checklist, and the flip is its own
release: both flags true, the clocks back to a week, the next origin
retired.

## Not in this brief

The flip itself. Standing-weighted order, partial pots, mid-cycle
joins (v2 non-goals). Real sats: the first real circle is after the
flip, small, among people Jet can reach, with the cold replay re-run
within a day.
