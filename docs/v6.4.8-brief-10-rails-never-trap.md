# Brief 10 — the funding card must never trap you on one rail

Blocks 6.4.8. Found by Jet, 2026-09-24, during the Lightning gateway outage:
with Lightning down there is no way to pick ecash, so ecash funding can't
even be tested. Verified against `38fd23f0`.

## What happens today

Brief 05 §5 made the card open straight into a Lightning invoice
(`autoLightning`, `AtomicFundingModal.tsx:210-214`). The rail chooser only
renders in the `choose-method` phase (`:569`). When the invoice can't be
created, the phase becomes `lock-failed` and renders `LockFailedState`
(`:724`) with exactly two buttons: **Try again** — which calls
`handleRegenerate` (`:459`), which starts Lightning again because
`autoLightning` is still true — and **Close**. There is no path to ecash,
on-chain or balance from there. During an outage the card is a loop.

## Fix

### 1. Rails on top, always

Put the rail toggle back at the **top** of the card, above everything else,
on every phase before a payment is detected — with Lightning pre-selected so
the QR still starts immediately below it. That keeps what Jet asked for
(pay at once) and what the original rail design had (you can always see and
switch). This supersedes brief 05 §5's "rails below the QR".

The live-invoice switch guard stays exactly as it is. Switching away from a
live invoice explains the wait; it never starts a competing run.

### 2. Failure hands you the other rails

When the Lightning request fails (`invoiceFailed`), the card shows the plain
failure line and the real reason, then the other rails directly underneath —
the same chooser, with Lightning marked *"Unavailable right now"* and not
selectable. **Try again** becomes a small secondary link. Never a state whose
only exits are the rail that just failed and Close.

### 3. Pre-flight decides the first rail

Brief 09 §A's gateway check runs when the card opens. With zero gateways,
Lightning is shown disabled with *"Lightning isn't available in this community
right now"* and the card opens on the first rail that works: balance (if it
covers trade + insurance), then ecash, then on-chain where supported.
`autoLightning` must include the pre-flight result.

### 4. Every rail says why when it's off

Disabled rails show their one-line reason — Lightning (no gateway / amount
floor), On-chain (web / under the minimum, brief 03 §E checkout), Balance
(not enough spendable). A greyed rail with no reason is a trap too.

## Tests

- Invoice failure ⇒ ecash and (where supported) on-chain are selectable from
  the failure state; Try again still retries Lightning.
- Zero gateways at open ⇒ no invoice request is made; the card opens on the
  first available rail.
- Rails render on top in `creating-invoice` and `awaiting-payment` phases.
- The live-invoice switch guard tests still pass unchanged.
