## G. The vote never wakes anyone (same bug as 6.4.14-02 B, other kinds)

Evidence, 2026-09-30 23:17: Bestie cast the second RELEASE vote on the
3,000-sat Market trade; the room went APPROVED ("Resolved"). Bitcrazy's
Pixel, swiped away, got nothing for many minutes. JOIN, LOCK and CHAT
wakes on the same trade all arrived.

Cause, from the code: `makeChainEventTagger` derives `w` tags from the
event's `p` (participant) tags. 6.4.15 C added peer `p` tags to JOIN
and CHAT. The VOTE builder (`escrow-client.ts` ≈2065) has none, and
neither do CLAIM (≈2135), COMPLETE (≈2448, ≈2495), CANCEL (≈2868) or
RESOLVE (≈4409). So a vote, a claim, a completion, a cancellation and
an arbiter ruling all publish without wake tags: the one moment that
says "your sats are ready" is silent on a closed phone.

Fix: every post-JOIN event names every other seated participant with
`p` tags (buyer, seller, arbiter minus self), exactly as LOCK already
does at ≈1910. One helper, `seatedPeerTags(state, me)`, used by all
builders. Tests: for each kind in VOTE, CLAIM, COMPLETE, CANCEL,
RESOLVE a signed event carries `w` tags for both peers (publish-wake
tests already do this for JOIN and CHAT; extend the table).

Then the wake job's copy for the winner: "✅ Your 3,000 sats are ready —
open Chama to claim" (`notify.approvedBody`, named per A), to the
non-winner "Resolved — the sats go to Bestie", and to the arbiter
nothing unless they ruled.
