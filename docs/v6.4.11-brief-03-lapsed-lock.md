# v6.4.11 brief 03 — A lapsed lock is not "your move"

Jet, 2026-09-28 (screens on `49f83c52`): the 28,000 on-chain listing
where Jetty never sent the deposit. The lock window ended; the room says
"You didn't lock in time, so your offer went back to the market. Nothing
was taken. [Post it again]" — right. But Home still shows "YOUR MOVE ·
1" and the bar "Needs you: 1" for it, forever. Commit; don't push or
ship. Last brief before 6.4.11 ships.

## A. Nothing at stake → nothing in the bar

`needsYouReason` / `selectNeedsYouTrades` (`decisions.ts:958+`): a trade
whose lock window lapsed with no sats locked and no deposit at the escrow
address is not attention. It is a listing that went back to Browse.
Exclude it from needs-you and from the attention pill; the "Post it
again" offer lives in the room and in Me → My trades as an ordinary row,
not as an alert. Same for a joiner whose hold lapsed.

The one exception is real money: an on-chain trade whose terms were
committed **and** whose address received a deposit (confirmed or seen)
after the window closed. That is needs-you for the funder — "A deposit
reached a lapsed trade — recover it" — routing to the refund/recovery
flow that already exists (`OnchainPayoutRecoveryCard`, refund leaf). The
observation comes from the same Esplora check the room uses; no deposit,
no alert.

## B. The reducer and the room agree on what "lapsed" means

Brief 05 C froze seats once funding terms are committed
(`getEffectiveParticipantAt`, `types.ts:~1297`) so a deposit in flight
can't lose its seats. This room shows committed terms (Bitcrazy seated)
**and** "lock window ended, offer went back to the market". Both can't be
true. Decide and make every surface follow one rule:

- Terms committed, **no deposit seen** by the end of the lock window →
  the trade lapses like any other: seats released, listing back to
  Browse, address retired (it is per-trade and never reused). The
  effective-participant freeze applies only while a deposit is seen or
  confirmed at the address.
- Terms committed, **deposit seen** → seats stay frozen, the room shows
  the deposit state, the LOCK publishes on confirmation as today.

Tests: lapsed + no deposit → not in needs-you, browsable again; lapsed +
deposit seen → needs-you for the funder with the recovery reason; the
reducer's effective participants match the room's copy in both cases.
