# Brief 05 — the guided flow, from Jet's 2026-09-23 walk-through

UX only. Do brief 03 first — none of this matters while two people can't
finish a trade. Verified against `2d613b3b`.

## 1. Offers: one card per listing, bracket visible

The offers screen showed two cards, **21 sats** and **500 sats**, both from
Strike, while the trade itself says *"Anything from 21 to 500 sats"*. First
establish whether those were one bracketed listing rendered twice or two
listings from one seller, and say which in the implementation note.

Either way the rule: **one card per listing, and the headline is the
bracket**, e.g. *21–500 sats · 0.02–0.43 USD*, not the minimum alone. A
bracket is choice inside one offer — that's the point Jet made: even one
seller gives you options. Badges (Best overall, Lowest price, Most trusted)
attach to the listing, not to an endpoint of its range.

## 2. Back goes where you came from

Opening an offer from the offers list and pressing back returns to **the
offers list**, with its search intact, never Home. The person picked an
Exchange offer; that's what they want to change their mind about.

General rule for this pass: every back control's label names its real
destination. The **Your move / bell** entry currently says "Home" and returns
you to wherever you were — make the label say where it actually goes, or make
it actually go Home. Label and behaviour must agree.

## 3. A way home from anywhere

Tapping the **Chama wordmark** in the header returns to the guided Home. It is
the convention every web user already knows, costs no screen space, and needs
no teaching. (Also: re-tapping the Browse tab while already in Browse resets
to the guided Home.) No double-tap gesture — nobody discovers those.

## 4. Show the loader while offers load

While matching runs, *"Checking live offers…"* sits at the bottom of the
screen and looks like a hang. Put `ChamaLoader` (the reduced-motion-aware
mark) where the results will appear, fully visible, until the first result or
a definite "no offers yet". Keep the small line for the live-updates state
after results exist.

## 5. Lightning first, immediately

On Fund & lock, open straight to the **Lightning QR** — no rail choice first.
The rail toggle stays, below the QR, for people who want on-chain or ecash.

Each visit to the funding card makes a **fresh invoice**. Leaving the card
stops watching it in the UI; the abandoned-invoice journal (6.4.7) already
records it. Do not claim the old invoice stops working — it doesn't. If
someone pays a stale one, the sats land in their own Chama wallet as balance
and show up in recovery; they are not lost. That can be said in one line on
the card: *"Paid an older request by mistake? It lands in your wallet."*

## 6. The arbiter record, for humans

Today: *"Arbiter record · Gentle Fish · 29400 sats / Healings: 1 · Disputes
voted: 1 · Median response: 3682 min · Bond tenure (blocks): — · Last signed
activity: 8/25/2026, 9:51:28 PM"*. Correct and unreadable, and it's the one
thing every trader should read before pressing Agree & join.

Lead with one plain verdict line, then the details collapsed:

- *"Gentle Fish has 29,400 sats at stake and has settled 1 dispute. Usually
  replies within 2–3 days."*
- *"Swift Cat is new here and has nothing at stake yet."* — amber, not red,
  not hidden.

Rules: a zero bond is said first, never buried; response time in days or
hours, never raw minutes; dates relative ("3 weeks ago").

**Same arbiter, different numbers on two devices.** Jetty's device showed
Gentle Fish with 6 healings and 9/5 last activity; Bestie's showed 1 healing
and 8/25. The record is computed from each device's local sample, which the
details already admit ("Local sample: 77 trades"). The verdict line must say
so — *"From what this device has seen"* — or two traders looking at the same
arbiter will read two different people.

**Names.** Use the person's nym everywhere, the renamed one when there is
one. Jetty named himself "Jetty" yet appears to Bestie as "Deft Fish". Brief
02's name cache fixes the lookup side; make sure the room strip, the record
and the notifications all read the same name for the same pubkey.

## 7. Notifications say the listing, not the id

*"A buyer is looking at your listing sm_mueorq2…"* — replace the id with the
listing's title (`notify.buyerInterestBody`, `trade-notifications.ts:295`).

(The duplicate Jet saw was a second origin — one via `getchama.app`, one via
`pleasant-talcum.local:51118`, a local dev instance signed in as the same
identity. Two origins, two service workers, two notifications. Not a bug.)

## 8. "Saving together" → "Circles"

Rename every user-facing occurrence, all four languages plus the landing.
"Chama" already means the savings group and is the app's name, so the
vertical can't also be called Chama; "Circles" is the word the rest of the
world uses and the one the code already uses (`chamaCircle`, `CircleCanvas`).

## 9. Circle creation — "How many of you?"

Jet's note: *"circle > how many of you? ⇒ not a one page canva."* Reading:
the seat-count question should be its own guided step — one question, one
screen — like the rest of the guided flow, rather than a field on a single
canvas. **Confirm this reading with Jet before building it.**
