## Long form — proposed release body

Chama 6.4.22 — clearer circles and recovery keys on friends' phones

Circle cards now put your own seat state beside the name: Seat held, Locked,
Collect now, or Claimed. A held seat is not presented as funded; the card still
shows how many members have collected below the title.

Browse filter taps bring results into view when they sit below the screen.
New matching listings arrive immediately while you are at the top. While you
read farther down, they wait behind a new-listings button. Category counts keep
up with arrivals outside the chosen shelf. Existing listing withdrawals and
stock changes still take effect immediately.

Creating an identity shows visible npub and recovery-key fields with one
Save & continue action. Submission never copies the key or claims that a password manager saved
the key. Me asks Did you save your key?, with Yes, I saved it and Copy key.
Copy key copies without opening the key; successful copying or your confirmation
records the backup receipt. The saved key lives under Me › Wallet; the unsaved reminder
is separate from that reveal. Saved sign-out is a light dialog; unconfirmed sign-out
displays its key inline.
The identity key does not restore browser-local bearer ecash.

Sign-in starts with First time here or Welcome home, each opening its own
flow. Language pills sit underneath so you can change language before signing in.
Returning users paste their saved recovery key and sign in automatically;
Enter works too. There is no paste button or public-name field. A named browser-
extension pill appears only when supported and asks for permission only on tap.
Extension sign-in asks only for the public key; wallet secrets and trade
messages wait for a deliberate action. Refused requests stop with a plain
retry sentence. Background renewal, external notification signing, automatic
premium operations, seed republishing and bond recovery wait for an explicit
path on extension accounts. NIP-07 uses separate encryption/signing requests,
so one popup for every complete money action cannot be guaranteed.

On iOS, an invoice’s Open in wallet uses a Lightning link with Copy beside it.
In the desktop Firefox/Bitwarden experiment, the control login offered Save but
the first visible Chama-field trial did not. The final Save & continue flow still
needs manager/device acceptance; no automatic-save guarantee is made.

Circle cards and Lock show who holds the shares: the federation and its guardian
count. Custom federations and configurations suggesting one operator carry
separate warnings before a seat is held and again before funding. Distinct iroh
nodes using one relay are not mistaken for a single guardian machine. Unknown
configuration stays unknown. These are custody disclosures, not safety guarantees.

New circles start at 1,000 sats each. New public circles have a bounded seat count
and a maximum derived from federation invoice and balance metadata; unavailable
limits prevent new public creation. Private-circle limits are advice. Existing committed
rotation-round terms and settlement remain unchanged. Native public previews
read configuration and metadata without opening or joining a wallet; the native
bridge must be rebuilt to provide this endpoint.

Federation join failures retain the SDK's underlying error, with sanitized
open/join and reachability diagnostics. A failed join stops the invoice spinner.
A fresh Safari identity joined successfully in the reported phone run; that
result does not establish why another identity failed. Existing wallet data is
preserved. Clearing Safari Website Data is not a safe general repair for this
error. The capability readout names the browser wallet's iroh relay dependency;
automatic fallback to a guardian's secure-WebSocket address is not guaranteed.

Escrow formats, settlement, wallet-reset and federation-switch safety policies
are unchanged. A rejected signer decrypt now stops the action rather than
being swallowed as an unreadable share or retried automatically. The replay changes remain in
6.4.20 and 6.4.21.

## Zapstore — proposed phone card

- See your circle seat’s status and who holds its shares before you lock.
- Start small: new circles begin at 1,000 sats each.
- Check whether your recovery key is saved, and find it easily in Me.
- Browse stays live, and new listings wait while you read.
