## Resolved overlaps

- Funding keeps the shared payment-details choice in the redesigned decision:
  matching saved handle, a newly saved private handle, or explicit chat fallback.
  An unchosen fiat payment method disables the hold. The choice reaches the
  modal, direct NWC and LOCK retry paths. The engine's preflight-only check and
  legacy already-spent recovery exception remain unchanged. The new inline
  payment field uses the same autofill suppression as existing payment fields.
- Vote sending feedback uses the signed `pendingVote` preview, removing the
  redesign's independent ten-second placeholder. Pending votes remain outside
  committed engine state, history, recovery and notification effects. They
  cannot offer another money action, a dispute presentation or a claim. Relay
  acknowledgement commits without waiting for echo; rejection/timeout restore
  the committed view.
- Buyer payment details appear once, before the payment question, using the
  redesign's typography and panel styling. The other participants retain the
  redesigned handle/method display.
- Manage merges local, recovered and verified announcements by bond address.
  Each expired, unspent bond remains a current row; only locally reclaimed
  records go into the redesign's collapsed history. A failed recovery still
  shows known remote rows with disabled actions and Retry. Per-bond announcement,
  reclaim/renew guards, recovery-only seed reads and the disabled per-bond writer
  flag remain unchanged. The duplicate announcement button was not restored.
