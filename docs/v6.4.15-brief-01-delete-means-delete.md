## A. Deleting a listing only hides it on this device (design bug)

Bitcrazy "deleted" ~10 listings; after reload they were back, "13 in
stock", and Bestie's Browse shows 12 of Bitcrazy's as OPEN. Cause:
"Clear unfunded listings" and every delete path call `retireListing(id)`
(`listing-renewal-ledger.ts:50`), which writes the id to a
**device-local** list. Nothing is published. Other devices, and this
device after a cache rebuild, still see the CREATE on the relays as
open. The renewal loop then republishes them, which is also what fired
six "New listing" wakes.

Rule: a delete is a signed, published **CANCEL** (kind 38107) by the
creator, and the reducer already takes a CANCEL on a CREATED listing to
terminal. Change every delete/clear path to publish CANCEL for each
listing, then retire locally. A cancelled listing never renews, never
appears in Browse or guided results on any device, and is not "in
stock". Keep the local retire only as the immediate UI hide while the
CANCEL propagates. Test: clear N listings → N CANCEL events on the
relay; a second device's Browse drops them on next sync; the renewal
loop skips cancelled ids.

Also: stock. "13 in stock" after clearing is the store's item stock
being re-derived from the resurrected listings. Once CANCEL lands, stock
follows.

## B. Wakes reach the phone; the wake job can't read the trade

Evidence, 2026-09-29 21:31 (Jet): Bestie joined a Bitcrazy listing while
Chama was swiped away. The Pixel got **"There may be new activity. Open
Chama to check."** — the generic fallback — plus, 2 min later, the
foreground-style "👀 A buyer is looking at your listing…". Then Bestie
sent several chats: nothing. Community wakes ("New listing…") arrive
fine.

Read that carefully: the pair tag for JOIN **matched and woke the
phone**. The wake job (brief 6.4.11-01 A) then failed or timed out and
fell back to the generic line — it could not turn the wake into "Bestie
joined your 170-sat offer". And the CHAT wakes either never arrived or
arrived and posted nothing. Two separate faults, neither in delivery:

1. **The wake job.** Alert log first: for the 21:31 wake, what did the
   job report — timeout, relay unreachable, "nothing new", or an error?
   Likely causes: the bounded job (≤ 8 s) can't finish a relay read +
   replay for a 57-trade identity on a cold process; or it reads only
   the cached snapshot (`cacheNativeWake`) which was written before the
   JOIN and so sees nothing new; or `needsYouReasonFor` has no reason for
   "someone joined your listing" (that's the seller's move: lock). Fix
   in order: the job fetches only events newer than the snapshot for the
   trade ids in the wake's tags (the `w` tag → escrow id map is in the
   snapshot), not the whole history; a JOIN on my listing is a reason
   ("Bestie joined your 170-sat offer — lock it"); a CHAT is a reason
   ("Bestie: are you up?" — the message text, since it's decrypted
   locally); time budget 15 s with the generic line only on hard
   failure. Test: a wake carrying one `w` tag for a JOIN produces the
   named notification within the budget from a 60-trade snapshot.
2. **CHAT wakes.** Same evidence pass as before but for today's events:
   do Bestie's 21:3x CHATs carry `w` tags on 6.4.15? If yes and no wake
   arrived, the server-side dedupe (`chama-wake` collapse key / 5 s gate
   / same-tag suppression) is swallowing them — a CHAT after a JOIN on
   the same trade must still wake. If no tag, the CHAT builder fix
   didn't take.
3. Alert log gets: the tags this device last registered (short), and
   per wake: matched tag, job result, elapsed ms. Without this we are
   guessing again.
4. Renewal wakes post nothing; new listings are named and grouped
   (secondary).

## D. Stores follow the same presence rule as offers

Jet: no separate "Renew" nags for stores. A storefront is an offer like
any other: it stays live while its seller uses Chama (the existing
presence rule from "Keep my offers live"), lapses when they're away,
and comes back when they return — bonded or not. Remove the "N of your
stores lapsed · Renew" block; the Keep-my-offers-live toggle covers
stores. A store the seller no longer wants is deleted (A). If spam ever
becomes a problem, a daily presence check is the lever, not a bond.

## E. Browse shows other communities' currency

Bestie, community BLF (USA · USD), sees "Sats à vendre · EUR 1 · Revtag
(Revolut) · PayPal" under Exchange with "All 38" selected. "All" today
means every community on the relay; a EUR offer is useless to someone
paying in USD. Rule: Browse and guided results show listings whose
`fiatCurrency` matches the viewer's community currency, in every scope;
"All" widens the community, never the currency. Other currencies live
behind one explicit chip ("Other currencies · 3") that is off by
default. Cards keep the flag + currency badge. Test: a EUR listing is
absent from a USD viewer's default Browse and guided results, present
under the Other-currencies chip.

### B, second pass — what the Pixel's Alert log says (2026-09-30)

Read with `docs/v6.4.15-brief-01-B-evidence-before-code.md`. Three facts,
then five things to do.

**Facts.**

1. `21:31:38 · unifiedpush · shown · replay failed · activity: shown`.
   The JOIN's `w` tag matched, ntfy delivered, the job ran and the
   replay threw. Delivery and matching are fine for pair tags. The
   generic line is the job's failure path, not a delivery fallback.
2. The four CHATs (21:32:19, :39, :43, :45, same tag `Y45MTRdPcIuvi0_P`)
   have **no receipt at all** in the log — not "foreground", not
   "rate-limited", nothing. The 5 s collapse gate cannot explain the
   first one (43 s after the JOIN). So for CHATs the loss is between the
   watcher's `wake()` and the app's UnifiedPush receiver, and we have no
   log on either side of that hop: the watcher logs only send *errors*,
   and the app logs only what reaches `onMessage`.
3. `21:47:13 ×2` and `21:28:30` are `foreground · not run`. Jet says
   Chama was swiped away at 21:47. `ChamaPushStore.foreground()` returns
   **true on timeout** (2 s wait on the main looper → "cannot establish
   background: do not double-alert"). On a cold process start from a
   push, the main thread is initialising the WebView, so a 2 s timeout
   is plausible and would mark a background wake as foreground. The
   9:06–9:07 burst was real foreground (Jet was in the app).

**Do.**

1. **Wake job (in progress, `wake-replay.ts`/`wake-entry.ts`).** Keep
   the direction: fetch only the trade ids behind the wake's tags from
   `since = cachedAt`, replay per trade, one bad trade can't veto the
   others, JOIN-on-my-listing and CHAT-with-text are reasons, result +
   elapsed reported. Two things it still needs:
   - The **watcher must send the matched tag** in the payload, for every
     transport (`{ wake: 1, sentAt, tag }` for UnifiedPush and Web Push,
     `data.tag` for FCM). Today the UnifiedPush payload is `{wake:1,
     sentAt}` and Web Push is empty, so `input.tags` is always empty and
     `wakeFilters` falls back to the whole `#p` history — the old path
     with a new name. The tag is already opaque; sending it back to the
     device that registered it leaks nothing.
   - Budget 15 s total; relay timeout 10 s is fine only if the job's
     outer wait moves from 8 s to 15 s too (`ChamaWakeJob`/worker), or
     the relay timeout fires after the job has already given up.
2. **Foreground check.** Timeout must mean *background*, not foreground:
   a wake that arrives while the activity is genuinely RESUMED will
   resolve in milliseconds; a 2 s stall means the process is cold. Also
   read `ProcessLifecycleOwner` (or the activity's `STARTED` state) rather
   than a `WeakReference<MainActivity>` that may be null-but-alive. Log
   which branch decided (`resumed` / `no activity` / `timeout`) in the
   Alert log entry so this never has to be guessed again.
3. **Two log lines for the missing hop.** Watcher: one line per send —
   `wake <tag[0..6]> <transport> <status> <ms>` (no endpoint, no event
   content). App: log every UnifiedPush `onMessage` receipt *before* the
   policy verdict, with the tag from the payload. After this, "CHAT wake
   never arrived" has exactly one place to look on each side.
4. **Alert log entry shape** (B3): `time · transport · tag[0..6] ·
   verdict (+ foreground branch) · job result · N trades · elapsed ms ·
   notification id`. And the header shows the count of registered tags
   and the last register HTTP status. Keep the last 50, not 20.
5. **Renewal wakes** (B4): a `renewal`-tagged CREATE on my own listing or
   on a listing I've seen posts nothing; new listings from others are
   named ("Bestie: coffee beans, 120k sats") and grouped under one
   summary notification.
