## A. The shared trade link says localhost (prod bug)

`share-link.ts:6` builds the link from `window.location.origin`. In the
APK that is `https://localhost`, so "Share" hands a friend
`https://localhost/?trade=sm_…`. Use the canonical public origin
(`https://getchama.app`) whenever the current origin is localhost, a
capacitor scheme, or a Start9 `.local`/onion host; keep the current
origin only for real web hosts. Test: native share → getchama.app link;
web share → same origin as the page.

## B. Background alerts arrive but mostly don't show

Facts from the Pixel: "Send a test alert" → Settings says received, **no
notification**. Trades from Bestie while Chama was swiped away: lock,
chat, approve → nothing; then one "Trade complete" with full text; then
nothing again. Safari PWA on the other device got everything. So the
wake reaches the phone and the job runs; what fails is the decision to
post. Two suspects, both in `ChamaWakePolicy.mayDisplay`
(`enabled && !foreground && now - lastWake >= 5000`):

1. **`foreground` is stale.** `MainActivity.onPause` sets it false, but
   after a swipe-away the process can outlive the activity with the flag
   in whatever state the last callback left it, and a process restarted
   by the push service starts with `foreground = false` only by luck of
   the static initialiser. Derive foreground from the activity lifecycle
   at wake time (`ProcessLifecycleOwner` / the activity's resumed state),
   never from a static written by callbacks.
2. **The 5-second gate and `setOnlyAlertOnce` on one fixed id (6501)**
   collapse a burst (lock → message → message → approve) into one silent
   update of the same notification. Per trade and per reason, one
   notification id; a new reason on the same trade replaces the text and
   alerts again; the 5 s gate applies per id, not globally.

Then make it diagnosable without adb: Settings → Background alerts gets
an **Alert log** (last 20 wakes on this device): time, transport, policy
verdict (foreground / rate-limited / notifications-disabled / shown),
job result (what changed, or "nothing new", or timeout), and the
notification id posted. "Send a test alert" always posts a visible
notification ("Test alert from your alert server — alerts reach this
phone") in addition to the Settings line. Jet reads the log and we stop
guessing.

Also check on the Pixel that the `chama_activity` channel isn't muted
(Android 13+ needs POST_NOTIFICATIONS granted; foreground local
notifications may use a different channel). Say in the Alert log if
`areNotificationsEnabled()` was false.

## C. Safari in Lockdown Mode can't open a dispute (or anything)

Screenshot: iPhone, Safari, Lockdown Mode on, trade LOCKED — the friend
taps Refund / dispute and gets "This browser does not support the
wallet's storage (OPFS). Try a recent Chrome, Edge, or Safari build."
(`sdk-adapter.ts:3304`). A vote is a signed Nostr event; it needs no
wallet. Two changes:

1. Never gate signed actions (vote, dispute, chat, join or cancel) on
   the Fedimint wallet being available. Funding with ecash, claiming as
   ecash, and ecash-to-Lightning payouts still need it. Audit every call site that boots the SDK
   before an action and remove it where the action doesn't spend or
   receive ecash.
2. When the wallet truly is needed and OPFS is missing, detect Lockdown
   Mode (WebAssembly / OPFS / `navigator.storage.getDirectory` absent on
   Safari) and say: "Safari's Lockdown Mode blocks the wallet's storage.
   Turn Lockdown off for getchama.app, or use Lightning / on-chain
   instead." Not "try a recent Safari build".

Implementation note — Codex correctly noted that ecash-to-Lightning
payouts still require the wallet: they redeem ecash locally before sending
Lightning. This is correct and intended, not an unfinished part of C.
Only signed actions were freed from the wallet requirement; choosing a
Lightning payout address does not remove the custody step.

## D. iOS Safari PWA: keyboard pushes the chat off-screen

Pixel is fixed (brief 01 C). On iOS the keyboard scrolls the page so the
bubbles disappear above; you must scroll to see them. Use
`visualViewport` resize/scroll events to size the trade room to the
visible height, pin the composer to `visualViewport.height`, keep the
message list scrolled to bottom on focus, and `overscroll-behavior:
contain` on the body so Safari doesn't move the whole page. Test in the
Safari PWA with the keyboard open: last bubble visible, composer visible.

## E. Every past trade in Me is dated today

Me → My trades: every card, including old broken ones, shows today's
date. The card is using a hydration/refresh time (or `Date.now()` as a
fallback) instead of the CREATE event's `created_at`. Fix the source;
add a test that a replayed 2026-09-21 trade renders 2026-09-21.

## F. QR: open with a wallet, not "Sharing text"

Long-press on the invoice QR opens Android's text share sheet. Jet wants
the wallet chooser. Native: tap **and** long-press on the QR / address →
`ACTION_VIEW` with the `lightning:` / `bitcoin:` URI so Fedi, Zeus,
Strike and friends appear; a small "Share" text link below does the
share sheet. If no app handles the scheme, fall back to copy with
"Copied — no wallet on this phone opens Lightning links".

## G. "A relay failed" under the QR while the lock succeeded

A brief red line under the QR says a relay failed to take the lock; the
lock went through on the others and the trade continued. Rule: if at
least one relay accepted, say nothing red — at most a muted "sent to 3
of 4 relays" that clears. Red only when no relay accepted, and then say
what to do (retry).

## H. Name your saved destinations

Saved Lightning addresses, LNURLs and NWC connections get an optional
label ("Fedi", "Alby", "Zeus at home") shown in "Your wallets" instead of
the raw string, with the raw value beneath in muted text. Rename and
remove from the same list. LNURLs without a label show shortened
(`lnurl1abc…xyz`) as today.
