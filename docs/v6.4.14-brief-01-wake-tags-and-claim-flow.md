# v6.4.14 brief 01 — Wake tags from every sender; a claim you can back out of

Jet, 2026-09-29, Pixel 8 on 6.4.13 (test alert: HTTP 204, notification
shown with the app swiped away) and iPhone Safari PWA. Commit each; don't
push or ship. A first.

## A. A counterparty wakes you whether or not *they* use alerts

`makeChainEventTagger` (`watch-tags.ts:64`) attaches no `w` tag when this
device's own Background alerts are off. So Bestie on the web, alerts off,
published lock, chat and approve with no tag; the watcher had nothing to
match; Jetty's phone — correctly registered, test alert working — was
never woken. Tagging is for the *other* party and costs an opaque hash.
Tag every event whenever `signer.conversationKey` exists; this device's
setting governs only what it registers, never what it tags. Also seed a
device's watches from the counterparty's first event on a trade (Bestie
joins → Jetty's phone watches), not only from own publishes. Tests: a
client with alerts off publishes a LOCK carrying one `w` tag per other
participant; the peer's registered tag matches it.

## B. Backing out of an ecash claim is not a claim

iPhone PWA, CLAIMED trade: tap Claim → Ecash → the ecash QR appears →
close the card without importing. The room then shows "Did the payout
reach your wallet? Confirm received", i.e. the trade advanced to CLAIMED
and Chama believes a note was handed out. Showing the QR must not be the
claim. Rule: the ecash note is minted and the CLAIM event published only
when the person taps "I've imported it" (or the app detects the import);
closing the card before that leaves the trade at APPROVED with nothing
minted. If a note *was* minted and the card is closed, keep it in the
recovery store and say so in the room ("An ecash note is waiting — open
Claim to show it again"), never "did the payout reach your wallet".
Same rule for Lightning: no CLAIM until the invoice is paid.

## C. Back inside the claim card

Claim card: choosing Lightning / On-chain / Ecash opens the next screen
with no way back except ×. Add a ‹ Back in the card header on every
sub-screen (rail chooser ← destination entry ← QR / confirmation),
keeping the state entered so far. × stays as "close the card". Same for
the funding card's sub-screens.

## D. Brackets on the review summary

"Review this match" shows "You receive 500 sats". For a range listing add
the bracket under it in muted text ("from a 21–60,000 sats offer") so the
buyer sees what they picked against what was offered. Cards keep brief
02 B (chosen amount first).
