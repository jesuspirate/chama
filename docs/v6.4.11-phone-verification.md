## A — bounded read-only native replay

A dedicated bundled entry point runs in an isolated WebView in an expedited
WorkManager job (API 31+), with a 7.8-second native wait budget. It reads a native
snapshot of signed cached events and the existing saved identity only for local
NIP-44 decryption, verifies incoming signatures, fetches relay history, replays
through the reducer and consults needsYouReasonFor. It never instantiates the
EscrowClient, MainActivity, a signer, or a wallet. Missing decryption material,
timeout and failed reads retain the generic fallback. Successful empty reads are
silent. Per-trade notifications share payout copy with foreground delivery and
carry native cold/warm trade links. Notification tags are deduplicated locally.
External signers without a locally saved key retain the generic fallback for
encrypted trades. Physical-device timing, process-death and tap checks remain.
