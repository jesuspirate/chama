# v6.4.10 brief 05 — What the first mainnet on-chain attempt hit

Jet, 2026-09-26, local build `0bcd18e8`: Jetty posted a 30,000-sat on-chain
exchange (Bitcrazy bonded 100k), Bestie joined from Browse. Jetty could not
fund it. Fix A and B first; they block the test. Commit each; don't push or
ship.

## A. The simple trade view funds every trade through Fedimint (blocker)

`LiveTradeSurface.tsx:290-297`: "Fund & lock" always calls `onLock`, which
opens `AtomicFundingModal` — Lightning selected, on-chain greyed ("Direct
on-chain is chosen when the offer is posted"), then "Couldn't lock the trade
— This trade uses on-chain escrow — fund the escrow address instead." It also
quoted 30,075 with "75 arbiter insurance", which is ecash math.

For `state.escrowMode === "onchain"` the lock decision must never reach the
Fedimint modal. Render the on-chain funding inside the simple view — commit
terms, QR/BIP21 with the exact amount, the waiting/seen/confirmed line, the
same `OnchainEscrowPanel` wiring TradeDetail uses (`TradeDetail.tsx:2229-2289`)
— or, at minimum, make the button open the full view scrolled to that panel.
Same for the buyer side: "Waiting for the seller to lock the funds…" should
say the deposit's real state once terms are committed.

Test: an on-chain trade in LiveTradeSurface never opens AtomicFundingModal.

## B. Guided results drop on-chain offers

`match-listings.ts:199` rejects with `FEDERATION_MISMATCH` whenever the
viewer has a federation and the listing's `mintUrl` differs. On-chain CREATEs
now carry `mintUrl: ""`, so every on-chain offer is filtered out (Bestie's
"Up to 30 USD" saw only the 3,183-sat offer, not Jetty's 30,000 for $25.20).
Skip the federation check and the `same_federation` score/reason for
`escrowMode === "onchain"`; they don't use a federation. Guided results
already group by rail (`groupBySettlementRail`), so they'll show under
on-chain.

Test: an on-chain listing with `mintUrl: ""` matches a viewer who has a
federation.

## C. When the funder's app is closed

Once funding terms are committed the seats freeze
(`getEffectiveParticipantAt`, `types.ts:1297`), so the 90-minute hold no
longer runs out and the deposit is safe at the escrow address whatever
happens. But the LOCK is published only by the funder's open app (the 20 s
poll). If Jetty sends and closes the app, Bestie waits with no signal until
Jetty opens Chama again.

1. The **buyer's** device checks the committed escrow address too (it can
   recompute it) and says the real state: "Jetty's deposit is confirmed on
   Bitcoin. It becomes the lock the next time Jetty opens Chama." Not a
   spinner.
2. The funder's app, on any launch, checks every trade with committed terms
   and no LOCK and publishes the LOCK without the trade page being open.
3. Evaluate (report, don't build yet): letting the **buyer** publish the
   LOCK once the deposit is confirmed at the committed address. The terms are
   already bound (brief 01 Part 1); a LOCK only records a fact anyone can
   check on-chain. If the reducer's funder-only rule can be relaxed safely for
   on-chain, the device-off gap disappears. Write what would have to change
   and what could go wrong.

## D. Arbiter record flickers and differs between devices

APK (GrapheneOS), Dashboard: the Arbiter record disappears and comes back;
"Bitcrazy has 100,000 sats at stake" sometimes becomes "doesn't have sats at
stake". Same trade, two devices: "Usually replies within 2 hours" vs "within
2 days".

Likely: a live bond read that comes back empty or fails chain verification
(Esplora hiccup) replaces the last verified set, and the record is rebuilt
from whatever this device has seen. Rule: **never downgrade on a failed or
empty read.** Keep the last chain-verified bond per arbiter until a newer
successful read shows it spent or expired. Find where the Dashboard and trade
view each get bonds and make them use the same cached verified set. For the
reply-time line, either compute it from the same event set on every device
or drop it until it can be; a number that differs by device isn't a fact.

## E. Dark mode shifts hue while the app hydrates

Jet: on load in dark mode the screen shifts slightly back and forth — a green
or orange tint. Not the OS. First suspect: the price hero's green/orange
gradient reacting to each median update (83,990 → 84,012 → 84,020) or a theme
token applied after first paint. Find what changes colour after first render,
make the first paint final, and don't animate tint on price ticks.
