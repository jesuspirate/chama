# Brief 08 — lock-from-balance must budget the insurance premium

Found reviewing the uncommitted 03→05 work. Fix before committing it.
Money path. Small.

## The gap

The fresh-payment path funds **trade + premium**: `useEscrow.ts` ~4816
computes `premiumMsats` (from `funderPremiumMsats`, passed in at
`App.tsx:4379`) and invoices `opts.amountMsats + premiumMsats`
(~4946/4952). The lock spends the trade amount; the premium stays in the
wallet as the residue the settle-time sweep pays to the arbiter. The funding
modal shows it to the user as insurance (`AtomicFundingModal.tsx:549`).

The new balance path never sees the premium:

- It runs at the top of the funding `try` (~4608), **before** `premiumMsats`
  is computed.
- `lockFromBalance` checks `canLockFromBalance(trade, spendable, amountMsats)`
  — trade amount only.
- The modal offers the button when `spendableMsats >= amountMsats`
  (`AtomicFundingModal.tsx:561`), and App passes spendable through only when
  `canLockFromBalance(…, pendingFundAndLock.amountMsats)` (`App.tsx:3517`).

So with a 100-sat trade, 10 sats of insurance and exactly 100 spendable, the
button appears, the lock succeeds, and at settle the premium sweep reaches for
10 sats that were never budgeted — out of balance another earmark owns, or it
fails and the arbiter goes unpaid.

## Fix

1. Compute the premium once, before the funding-method branch, and use
   **trade + premium** as the balance requirement in all three places:
   `canLockFromBalance`, the modal's offer condition, and App's pass-through.
2. The offer line names both: *"Use ₿ 110 of your ₿ 500 — 100 for the trade,
   10 insurance."* Zero premium reads as today.
3. The premium is not moved at lock time — it stays in the wallet exactly as
   in the invoice path. Only the requirement changes.

## Tests

- Spendable = trade exactly, premium > 0 ⇒ balance option not offered, and
  `lockFromBalance` refuses if called anyway.
- Spendable = trade + premium ⇒ offered, and after the lock the remaining
  spendable is at least the premium.
- Sim/testnet (premium forced to 0) behave as before.
