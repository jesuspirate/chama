## A. Wake copy must follow who locks

The 1,200-sat trade is a Market listing: the **buyer** locks
(`expectedLockerRole("marketplace") === BUYER`). Bitcrazy is the seller.
His phone said "Bestie joined your 1200-sat offer — **lock it**". That
is the opposite of his move; he waits while Bestie funds.

`wakeNotification` (`wake-replay.ts`) fires the JOIN line whenever
`reason === 'waiting'` and a buyer hold appeared — i.e. exactly when it
is *not* the recipient's move. Fix: the JOIN copy is chosen by
`expectedLockerRole(state.category)` against the recipient's role:

- recipient is the locker (Exchange, Bill Pay seller): "Bestie joined
  your 1,200-sat offer — lock it" (title "Your listing needs you").
- recipient is not the locker (Market seller): "Bestie joined your
  1,200-sat offer — waiting for her to lock" (title "Your listing",
  not "needs you"; it is a status, and the bar must not go needs-you).
- recipient is the arbiter: nothing at JOIN, unless the on-chain
  arbiter-key rule already applies.

Same rule in the foreground path (`notificationForTransition`): the
JOIN copy and the LOCKED copy ("the other side funded it. Your move")
must name the party and the amount, never the trade id: "Bestie locked
1,200 sats — send the item and mark it sent." The `{label}` short-id
strings in `notify.*` go. Tests: one signed JOIN chain per category
(p2p-trade, bill-pay, marketplace), notification asserted for seller,
buyer and arbiter recipients.

## B. Chats must not replace the state line

At 12:53 "Bestie: yoooo" replaced "⚡ Sats locked in escrow" on the
Pixel. `ChamaPushStore.post()` cancels `tradeNote:<trade>` whenever the
new notification id differs, so any chat kills the actionable line.
Rule: state notifications for a trade replace each other (one line per
trade, always the current state — that is right and stays). Chats are
their own lane: one messaging-style notification per trade
(`MessagingStyle`, sender name, last N lines), grouped with the state
line under the trade, never cancelling it. Marking the trade read in
the app clears both. Test on the Pixel: lock, then two chats — the
shade shows "Sats locked" and one chat card with two lines.

## E. Funding card: the rail is a fact, not a tab bar

Jet: "I don't see a scenario where the user can freely change their
selection of rail." He's right, and the code agrees. In
`AtomicFundingModal` the row is Lightning · On-chain · Ecash, but
On-chain is *always* disabled on an ecash trade ("chosen when the offer
is posted") and an on-chain trade shows the single `["onchain"]` rail.
"Ecash" is not a rail either, it is "lock from my Chama balance", and
it is only possible when `hasBalance`. So the only real fork is
Lightning vs. wallet balance, only when the balance covers the amount,
and the card creates the invoice on open, which locks the row before
the user could ever pick. The tabs are theater; `lockedRail`,
`switchRequested` and `fund.railLocked` exist to explain the theater.

Replace the row with a decision made before paint (6.4.15 G already
put the decision there):

- On-chain trade → the on-chain address card. No rails shown.
- Ecash trade, spendable balance < amount → the Lightning invoice
  card. No rails shown. One muted line only if the user has *some*
  balance: "Your Chama wallet has 4 sats spendable; this trade needs
  1,200 — paying by Lightning."
- Ecash trade, spendable balance ≥ amount → one question **before any
  invoice exists**: "Pay 1,200 sats from your Chama wallet (1,540
  spendable)?" with two buttons, "Use my wallet" / "Pay by Lightning".
  Then paint the chosen card. No switching afterwards: to change, the
  user cancels the card (Back), which is the same cancel as today and
  keeps the late-payment watch on the invoice (6.4.15 B).

Delete `PaymentRails` from the funding card, `lockedRail`,
`switchRequested`, `fund.railLocked`, `fund.onchainAppOnly` and the

## H. Two on-chains, two names (Jet: "I fully agree")

Direct escrow and federation peg-in both say "On-chain" today, and both
end in a QR and a paste box, so they read as one thing. They are not:
the direct escrow holds the sats on Bitcoin in a 2-of-3 Taproot output
nobody custodies; the peg-in moves sats *into the federation*, where
they become ecash, and only then does the ordinary ecash lock happen.
Naming rule, everywhere the rails appear (`PaymentRails`, the funding
card, the claim modal, the on-chain overlay, the guided view):

- Direct escrow rail: **Bitcoin**. Sub-line: "Held on Bitcoin · 2-of-3
  · no custodian." The other rails on an on-chain trade keep "This
  trade is held on Bitcoin."
- Federation deposit rail (native only): **Deposit to {federation
  display name}** ("Deposit to Bitcoin Life Federation"). Sub-line:
  "fee {pegInFeeSats} sats · becomes ecash after {finalityDelay}
  confirmations". Browser: rail hidden (E), and where a reason is
  needed, "On-chain deposits need the Chama app" replaces
  `fund.onchainAppOnly`'s current text.
- Claim side: direct trade → "Payout address" (kicker "ONCHAIN CLAIM"
  stays, blurb unchanged); ecash trade peg-out → "Withdraw from
  {federation} to a bitcoin address", with the peg-out fee on the line.

`payment.onchain` splits into `payment.bitcoin` (direct) and
`payment.federationDeposit` with the name interpolated; four languages.
Tests: an on-chain trade never renders "Deposit to"; an ecash trade on
native never renders "Bitcoin" as a rail label.
