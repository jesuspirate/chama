## A. A buyer that missed the terms never catches up (blocker)

`escrow-client.ts:3670-3685`: an incoming event that fails to apply triggers
a full relay reload only for `INVALID_STATE`, `NOT_PARTICIPANT`,
`THRESHOLD_NOT_MET`. Every code Part 1 added — `INVALID_ONCHAIN_LOCK`
("Funding terms were not published before LOCK"), `FUNDING_TERMS_FROZEN`,
`INVALID_FUNDING_TERMS` — is treated as permanent and only `console.debug`'d.
So if the LOCK reaches a device before the seller's terms JOIN (two relays,
two orders), the LOCK is dropped for good; the trade shows CREATED until a
reload happens to replay the chain in order.

1. Any rejection whose cause can be a missing predecessor arms
   `scheduleOutOfOrderReload`: the three on-chain codes above, plus
   `ARBITER_PUBKEY_MISMATCH` and `MISSING_ARBITER_PUBKEY` on an on-chain
   trade (the arbiter is seated by the terms JOIN).
2. The simple view says when the chain is being re-fetched, and shows
   `state.replayNotes` / rejections the same way the full view does
   (`LiveTradeSurface.tsx:838` only renders notes in one branch).
3. Find why Bestie's device had **no terms either**. Jet will attach her
   console (`[escrow] Rejected event …` lines) and
   `npx tsx scripts/inspect-trade-relays.ts sm_muir4bz7_4pn9jfv1`. Check
   the seller's terms JOIN against her local state: hold expiry at the
   event's timestamp, `escrowKeys`, arbiter pick. If the gate can disagree
   between two devices that hold the same chain, that is the bug to fix,
   not the symptom.
4. Test: deliver LOCK before the terms JOIN to a fresh client; after the
   reload the state is LOCKED. Deliver terms JOIN, then LOCK, then a
   duplicate LOCK: no reload storm.
