# v6.4.10 brief 07 — After the first mainnet settlement

Commit each; don't push or ship.

## A. Settlement: the winner chooses, then everyone signs

Seen on `9f51d3d9` at APPROVED: the guided view offered "Claim them into
your wallet" → `ClaimPayoutModal` → "Claim did not settle — this escrow is
held on-chain". `c77376b3` replaces that decision with "Finish the Bitcoin
settlement"; keep it that way and add a test: an on-chain trade never opens
`ClaimPayoutModal` or `AtomicFundingModal`, from either view.

`OnchainTradeControls.tsx:93-102`: every eligible signer auto-runs
`onPrepareOnchainSettlement(id)` on mount, which builds and **publishes** a
PSBT to the per-trade fallback key before the winner has said where they
want the sats. Two devices, two PSBTs, and the winner's later direct
address makes a third. Change: only the winner prepares, and only after
they either enter an address or tap "Send to my Chama key instead". The
other signer sees "Waiting for <winner> to choose where the sats go", then
"Sign the payout" once a winner-authored PSBT exists. One PSBT per trade
unless the winner changes the destination before anyone else signs.

USD" (currency from the trade/community) listing the fiat-out methods
available for that country — Strike now, Tando/ChapSmart where they apply —
each as a card like the Strike one, always visible when at least one
applies, not behind a disclosure. NWC and saved wallets go under "Your
wallets". "Details" as a label goes away.

still being read from relays; Me → My trades has its own loading behaviour.
