## A. The invoice outlives the seat (money loss)

Bitcrazy (seller) opened the funding card: "Waiting for payment · 14:33"
(an invoice with ~15 min left). He paid 170 sats from Fedi ("You sent
170 SATS"). The card then said "Local apply failed: LOCK must wait for
the buyer to finalize the order" — the buyer's join hold had expired
(`JOIN_HOLD_SECONDS` is 5 min + 2 min grace, `types.ts:90-91`;
`state-machine.ts:1058-1068`, `ORDER_NOT_FINALIZED`). The 170 sats sit
in Bitcrazy's Chama wallet as ecash, not in escrow, and the trade shows
nothing. Two clocks, and the longer one was the invoice.

Rule: **nothing can be paid into a seat that can lapse before the LOCK
applies.**

1. The funding invoice's expiry is min(gateway default, the seat's hold
   expiry + grace − 60 s) — never longer than the window in which the
   LOCK can still apply. Same for the on-chain funding card: the
   "prepare" step refuses if the hold has under N minutes left and says
   so.
2. The card's countdown is the **seat** countdown, not the invoice's:
   "Bestie's seat holds for 3:12 — pay before then". At 0 the card
   closes the invoice (cancel the receive operation), shows "The buyer's
   seat lapsed before you paid. Nothing was taken." and offers Post it
   again / Wait for them to rejoin.
3. Before building the LOCK, re-run the reducer's precondition
   (`ORDER_NOT_FINALIZED`, hold expiry, seats) **against the current
   state** and refuse to create an invoice at all if a LOCK could not
   apply right now. The invoice is created only for a trade that could
   lock this second.
4. If, despite this, a payment lands and the LOCK is refused: the sats
   are already ecash in the funder's wallet (Fedimint credited them).
   Say exactly that, in the card and in the room: "Your 170 sats are in
   your Chama wallet, not in escrow. The buyer's seat lapsed. Post it
   again to try once more." Never "Local apply failed: …". And write a
   recovery row (brief 6.4.8-07 `lock-recovery` kind) so the bar shows
   it until the person has seen the sats in Wallet.
5. Same audit for every other "money moved, event refused" seam: LOCK
   after listing expiry, LOCK with a stale arbiter pick, CLAIM after
   the trade was cancelled. Each must either be prevented before payment
   or explained with where the sats are.

Tests: an invoice built with 6 min of hold left expires in ≤ 6 min; a
hold that lapses closes the card and cancels the receive; a payment that
lands after lapse produces the wallet copy and a recovery row, and the
reducer error text never reaches the screen.
