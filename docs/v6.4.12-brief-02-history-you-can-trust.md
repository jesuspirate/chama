## A. A chat after the end must not hide the whole trade

`https://getchama.app/?trade=sm_muj42kml_a29dcuk7` — a disputed trade,
refund voted — opens on the phone as "This trade's history couldn't be
rebuilt on this device … (TRADE_CLOSED · bd6505…)". The reducer refuses a
CHAT sent after the trade reached a terminal state
(`state-machine.ts:1756`), and the replay treats that refusal as a failed
rebuild, so the room falls back to the local summary and the trade
"disappears".

A late chat is not a broken chain. Rule: rejections of **non-state**
events (CHAT after close, a duplicate VOTE, a second COMPLETE, an
arbiter's late vote on a settled trade) become replay notes and leave
the rebuilt state intact; only a rejection that breaks the **state**
chain (a LOCK the reducer can't accept, a missing predecessor) marks the
rebuild as failed. `needsTradeHistory` / `stateProvenance`
(`decisions.ts:513`) follow the same split. A late chat may even be
shown, greyed, under "after the trade closed".

Test: a completed trade + one CHAT after COMPLETE replays to COMPLETED
with a note, room opens normally; a chain missing its LOCK still reports
"couldn't be rebuilt".

## B. Cards show what was traded, not the bracket

Me → My trades and Browse cards for a locked or finished range listing
show "21–500" or "21–60,000". Once a buyer chose an amount (JOIN with a
finalized order, or LOCK), the card shows that amount — "3,183 sats"
— with the bracket only in the room's details. The bracket stays on
cards only for listings still open, or closed with no order ever
chosen.

## C. Rows this device can't vouch for are not listed as trades

Bitcrazy's My trades lists "testing – v0.1.30 subbbbbbbb" dated Sep 27
with a 63-sat amount and "0/3" — a fossil from months ago, no chain on
any relay, a cached summary with a refresh date. Brief 01 E fixed dates
where a signed CREATE exists; this row has none.

Rule: a row appears under My trades only if this device holds the signed
CREATE for it (then its date is that event's). Everything else — summary-
only, no CREATE, provenance "summary" — moves to a collapsed "Unverified
history (N)" section at the bottom with no date, no amount and no status
badge, just the id and "This device has no signed record of this trade."
Counts in the header ("59") exclude them. Same for Browse: a listing with
no CREATE this device verified is never shown as open.

Also: a trade whose CREATE is older than the relay retention window and
that this device never cached should say so in that section ("older
than what relays keep") rather than pretend. Row 2 of the 6.5 doc is
about this; here we only stop lying.

## D. The Android text-selection popup is dark on a light app

GrapheneOS, light mode: long-press in a field shows "Paste ⋮" in a black
box. That is the WebView's action-mode popup following the system dark
theme. Make the WebView's `ActionMode` / floating toolbar theme follow
the app theme (Capacitor: set the activity theme's `actionModeStyle` and
`isLightTheme`, or `WebSettingsCompat.setForceDark` off with a light
`android:theme` on the activity when the app is light). Check both
modes.

## E. Background alerts never register

`ChamaPushStore.send` refuses to register with empty tags. Tags are only
created by `makeChainEventTagger` on this device's own publishes, and
disable wipes both tags and endpoint. A fresh enable registers nothing,
so `/test` returns HTTP 404, reported in the app as "No reply".

Register the endpoint on enable with `tags: []` (the VPS accepts it).
Seed tags on enable and every launch from all open trades this identity
participates in. Surface `/test`'s HTTP status in the Alert log and the
Settings line. Keep the endpoint across a toggle when the distributor
allows, while removing watch registrations and suppressing delivery when off.

Test: fresh enable registers with no trades; existing participant trades
are watched without publishing; toggling off/on reuses a valid endpoint;
HTTP 404 is distinguished from a successful request with no delivery.
