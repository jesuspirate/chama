# Hero film prompt

Create a 15-second cinematic narrative film, a visual poem about rotating community savings, NOT an idle portrait or looping smiles. Use the supplied image to preserve the same four adult friends, their exact faces, clothing, woven bowl and warm courtyard. Real photographic textures, purposeful hands, calm dignity, sunlit amber palette. Widescreen cinematic composition, medium wide enough to understand actions. Clear orange Bitcoin tokens bearing a simple white Bitcoin B symbol are symbolic representations of sats, not piles of gold wealth. Phone screens must be visibly ON with simple bright orange Bitcoin symbols and a small lock symbol, not blank black screens. No printed text or subtitles.
SHOT 1, 0-4 seconds: move from a close view of a woman's thumb pressing a lit phone with an orange Bitcoin symbol, then glide to the center of the round table. Three contributors each place an equal small orange Bitcoin token into the woven bowl in sequence. Every placement makes a restrained amber rim pulse. Hands and contribution action are clearly visible; the fourth person waits to receive.
SHOT 2, 4-9 seconds: match cut later, warm Sunday-like light. The bowl now contains the contributions, clearly visible orange Bitcoin tokens. The group gently slides it toward the woman wearing the patterned headwrap. She reaches with both hands, receives the filled bowl, looks down in relieved delight, then looks up to warmly thank the friends. This is the emotional payoff, a meaningful received turn, not generic posing.
SHOT 3, 9-15 seconds: a graceful time-passing match cut begins a NEW round. The same woman who just received now contributes an orange Bitcoin token herself; the other members contribute equal tokens. This time the filled bowl moves toward the older grey-haired man. He receives it with a small moved smile. End on the entire group and the filled bowl in his hands, an unmistakable continuing circle of reciprocity. Hold final composition one second. Natural positive emotion only as a consequence of action. No backwards motion, no boomerang, no idle smile reset, no morphing, no extra fingers, no faceless hands, no cash, no coins multiplying without contributions, no logos except Bitcoin symbols. Silent, no speech, no soundtrack.
