# Digital community scene

Built-in image generation. Prompt: contemporary adult friends around ages 25–40 using their own smartphones at a warm urban café. Natural shared smiles, candid editorial photography, modern casual clothes, realistic hands. Leave negative space for authored phone UI. No physical coins, basket, cash, invented app interfaces, logos or text.

The phone demonstration is HTML/CSS with translated text and the native five-seat Circle SVG. Ready → collect → received is an illustrated prepared claim, not a promise that every payout bypasses wallet setup or settlement. It never sends a transaction.
