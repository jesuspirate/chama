A closer look at the safeguards↗
Two signatures. One settlement.

Buyer, seller, and arbiter each have a role. Two of the three must agree to settle an escrow trade.

The terms come first.

Know the amount, the conditions, and the arbiter before you lock sats. Start in the sandbox to learn the flow.
