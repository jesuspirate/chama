How it works

One finds a need. Two make a trade. Three make sure it settles.

The third person does not run the trade. They make sure neither side can hold it hostage.

1

Agree

Choose the terms, the amount, and a community arbiter you both trust.

2

Lock

The sats lock into 2-of-3 escrow. Nobody—not even Chama—controls them alone.

3

Settle

Both sides confirm and funds release. If there is a dispute, the arbiter breaks the tie.

Why it’s different

The trade belongs to its people.

Buyer, seller, and arbiter share one path to settlement—and the outcome belongs to them.

Chama canPublish the offer, relay signatures, and show the outcome.
Chama cannotTake the sats, act alone, or rewrite what happened.
