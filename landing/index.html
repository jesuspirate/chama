How it works

Three people. One honest trade.

Most trades finish with the buyer and seller alone. The arbiter only steps in when they disagree.

1

Agree

Choose the terms, the amount, and a community arbiter you both trust.

2

Lock

The sats lock into 2-of-3 escrow. Nobody—not even Chama—controls them alone.

3

Settle

Both sides confirm and funds release. If there is a dispute, the arbiter breaks the tie.

Why it’s different

The platform never becomes the bank.

Chama coordinates the agreement. It does not own the money, choose the winner, or keep a private ledger of truth.

✓

2-of-3 escrow means nobody acts alone.

✓

Nostr-native identity makes reputation portable.

✓

Open source rules can be inspected and replayed.
